I joined the Computational Neuroscience Research Group (CNRG) at the University of Waterloo in September 2024, and earned an MMath in Computer Science in August 2026.
Prior to that, I earned a BSc in Computer Engineering at the University of Alberta in April 2024.

I am broadly interested in how humans reason effectively and efficiently.
My masters thesis research primarily concerned the [Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General Intelligence (ARC-AGI)](https://arcprize.org), an extremely difficult&mdash;and largely unsaturated&mdash;benchmark that tests fluid rather than crystalline intelligence.
In addition to my masters thesis, I published multiple papers on this topic, including one that won a paper award in the [ARC Prize 2025 Competition](https://arcprize.org/competitions/2025).
